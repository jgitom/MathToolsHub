Managing your subscription

Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

Cancel at any time

Subscribers can cancel through User Account → Manage billing. Stripe securely handles cancellation and billing details.

Access after cancellation

Cancellation stops future renewal. Premium access normally remains available until the end of the already-paid billing period. The account page shows the access end date.

No automatic prorating

Unused time is not automatically prorated. Refund requests are considered under the Refund Policy and applicable consumer law.

Payment failure

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe may retry the payment and send billing notices. Premium access may be restricted until payment is successfully completed.

Deleting an account

Deleting or abandoning an account does not automatically cancel a Stripe subscription. Cancel billing first through the Customer Portal, then email admin@mathtoolshub.com if you also want the account deleted.
