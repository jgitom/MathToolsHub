Barcode Scanner

Compatible with any barcode scanner — USB, Bluetooth, and 2.4 GHz wireless models that emulate a keyboard (HID). Common brands (Zebra, Honeywell, Socket Mobile, Wasp, etc.) work out of the box.

  • Plug the scanner in and it works immediately — no driver installation needed.
  • Scans are detected even when the search box is not focused.
  • Enter, Tab, or no-suffix scanners are all supported automatically.
  • Auto-commits when a barcode is scanned; beeps and shows feedback on the Point of Sale page.
  • Manual entry also works — click ⌨ Scan or type a SKU and press Enter.

If your scanner appends extra characters, most models can be configured in their setup manual (usually by scanning a "suffix none" or "enter suffix" barcode). The app already strips common suffixes such as ;, _, :, and /.
